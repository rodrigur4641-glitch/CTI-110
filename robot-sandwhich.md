# Lets Make A Sandwhich 

## Assumptions
The robot will have the following ingredients and tools to perform the task of making a PBJ sandwhich: 
- 2 slices of bread 
- jar of peanut butter 
- jar of grape jelly 
- 1 butter knife 
- 1 plate 

## Step-By-Step
1. Pick up the peanut butter jar and open the lid
2. Place the lid down 
3. Pick up the the knife 
4. With the knife scoope out a quater size amount of peanut butter out of the jar
5. Put the jar down 
6. With the knife spread the peanut butter on one slice of bread
7. Use a paper towel to clean the knife (this is optional)
8. place the knife down
9. Pick up the grape jelly jar and open the lid
10. Place the lid down 
11. Pick up the knife
12. With the knife scoope out a quarter size amount of the grape jelly
13. Put the grape jelly jar down 
14. With the knife spread the grape jelly on the other slice of bread 
15. Put the knife down 
16. Put the two slices of bread together on the plate

## Done When
- Both slices of bread come together on the plate
